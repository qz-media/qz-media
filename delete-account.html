To request deletion of your QuizGo account and associated data, please send an email request to the address below using the email address registered with your account. After a valid account deletion request is processed, we will take reasonable steps to delete or anonymize information associated with your account where required and technically feasible.

Certain information may need to be retained for a limited period where reasonably necessary for:

Where information is retained for these purposes, it will be handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy and applicable requirements.
