This Privacy Policy explains how QuizGo ("QuizGo", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the QuizGo mobile application (the "App").

By using QuizGo, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not use the App.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use QuizGo, we may collect or process the following categories of information.

1.1 Account and Authentication Information

When you create or access a QuizGo account, we may collect information such as:

  • Mobile phone number
  • Firebase authentication identifier
  • Name or display name
  • Account creation date
  • Account status
  • Referral code
  • Referral relationship information
  • Other information necessary to maintain your account

Phone authentication may be provided through Firebase Authentication.

We use this information to create and maintain your account, authenticate you, prevent unauthorized access, and provide App features.

1.2 Quiz and Activity Information

When you use QuizGo, we may collect information related to your activity within the App, including:

  • Questions attempted
  • Answers submitted
  • Whether an answer was correct
  • Points awarded
  • Quiz activity
  • Point history
  • Referral activity
  • Account activity related to the QuizGo system

This information is used to operate the quiz system, calculate points, maintain records, detect abuse, and provide App features.

1.3 Referral Information

QuizGo may provide a referral system.

If you participate in the referral system, we may collect and store information such as:

  • Your referral code
  • The referral code used during registration
  • The user associated with a referral
  • Referral relationships
  • Referral-related activity
  • Eligibility information

1.4 Payment Information

QuizGo may allow users to submit payment-related information for the purpose of facilitating payments that QuizGo may choose to make outside the App.

Depending on the payment method selected, this information may include:

  • Account holder name
  • Mobile number or account number
  • Selected payment method
  • Other information reasonably necessary to facilitate or verify a payment

QuizGo does not guarantee that submitting payment information or having a particular number of points will result in a payment.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information collected through QuizGo for purposes including:

  • Creating and maintaining user accounts
  • Authenticating users
  • Providing quiz functionality
  • Recording quiz answers
  • Awarding and maintaining points
  • Operating the referral system
  • Verifying eligibility for applicable benefits
  • Processing or facilitating payments when applicable
  • Preventing fraud, abuse, cheating, and multiple-account misuse
  • Protecting the security of the App
  • Troubleshooting technical problems
  • Improving the App
  • Maintaining records
  • Communicating with users when necessary
  • Complying with applicable legal requirements
  • Operating advertising services
  • Measuring advertising performance

Points are not a bank balance, deposit, salary, or guaranteed payment entitlement.

Any promotional, discretionary, or manual payment that QuizGo may choose to make is subject to the applicable rules, verification, availability, fraud checks, and other conditions.
