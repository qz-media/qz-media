Account Deletion Information

You can request deletion of your QuizGo account and associated account information.

How to Delete Your Account

To request account deletion, use the account deletion option available inside the QuizGo app.

If you are unable to access the account deletion option inside the app, you may contact us using the email address below.

App: QuizGo

Email: qz.mediaofficial@gmail.com

What Happens After Deletion?

After a valid account deletion request is processed, we will take reasonable steps to delete or anonymize information associated with your account where required and technically feasible.

Account deletion may permanently remove access to:

  • Your QuizGo account
  • Quiz history
  • Points records
  • Referral information
  • Other account-related features

Information That May Be Retained

Certain information may need to be retained for a limited period where reasonably necessary for:

  • Fraud prevention
  • Security
  • Legal compliance
  • Financial or transaction records
  • Dispute resolution
  • Enforcement of applicable agreements

Where information is retained for these purposes, it will be handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy and applicable requirements.
